A briefing document: what someone needs to know before a meeting about these
sources, in the order they need it.

# ‹Title›

One line naming what the documents are about. Not "Briefing Doc".

## The short version

Three to five sentences that answer "what is this and why does it matter". If
the reader stops here, they should still be able to hold a conversation.

## What the sources say

The substance, in sections with their own headings. One heading per subject the
documents actually treat, in the order that makes sense to read, not in the
order the documents happen to be in. Cite the passages the claims rest on.

## Where the sources disagree

Every contradiction, each with both positions and both citations. If there is
none, write one sentence saying so; do not manufacture tension.

## What is not in the sources

The questions a reader will have after reading this that these documents do not
answer. This section is the honest one, and it is short.
