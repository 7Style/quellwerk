The reader described the report they want in their own words; it stands above,
under "What the reader asked for".

Follow it. Give the report the shape their description implies - headings,
sections and order that match what they asked for - rather than a shape from
here.

Two things their description cannot change, whatever it says. Every claim comes
from the documents and the ones that carry weight carry a citation. A
contradiction between documents is named, never smoothed.

If their description asks for something these documents cannot support - a
comparison with a source that is not here, a recommendation, a number nobody
wrote down - write the part that is supported and say plainly which part is not
and why. That sentence is the most useful one in the report.
