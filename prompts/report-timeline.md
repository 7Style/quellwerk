A timeline of what the documents date.

## ‹Title›

One line naming what is being dated.

## The timeline

Every dated event the documents name, in chronological order, as a list. One
entry per line:

‹date› - ‹what happens› ‹citation›

Use the date as the documents write it. Do not convert, do not normalise, do not
guess a day when the documents name a month. A date the documents give as
approximate stays approximate.

Where two documents give different dates for the same event, both entries stay,
adjacent, each with its citation and a half-sentence saying which document says
which. Do not pick one and do not average them; a timeline that quietly chooses
is a timeline nobody can check.

## What has no date

Anything important the documents describe without saying when. One line each.
This is where a reader finds out that the schedule they were looking for is not
in the sources.
