A study guide: what to learn from these sources, and how to check whether it
stuck.

## ‹Title›

One line naming the subject.

## What you will be able to do

Four to six statements, each beginning with a verb, describing what a reader who
has worked through this guide can do. Not "understand X": name the thing they
can do with X.

## The key terms

Every term the documents define or rely on, each with the definition the
documents give and a citation. A term the documents use without defining belongs
here too, marked as such.

## The material

The substance in sections, ordered so that each one only needs what came before
it. Cite as you go.

## Questions to test yourself

Six to ten questions that are answerable from these documents alone, from easy
to hard. Each one with a short answer and the citation it comes from. A question
whose answer is not in the sources does not belong in a study guide for them.
