Frequently asked questions, answered from these documents.

## ‹Title›

One line naming the subject.

Then the questions, each as a heading of its own, with the answer underneath.

Between eight and fifteen of them. The questions are the ones a reader of these
documents would actually ask - the practical ones, the ones about numbers and
dates, the ones where the documents are easy to misread. Not the ones that make
the answer easy.

Every answer is two to five sentences and carries the citation it rests on. An
answer that needs a caveat gets the caveat in the same breath, not in a footnote.

Order them the way someone learning the subject would ask them: what it is,
then who it applies to, then what it requires, then what happens if it is not
followed.

Where the documents disagree about an answer, the question gets both, marked as
such, with both citations.
